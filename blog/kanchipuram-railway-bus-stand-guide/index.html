Kanchipuram Railway Station and Bus Stand Guide

Whether you're arriving by train or bus, here's what to expect at Kanchipuram's railway station and bus stand, and how to get into town from either one.

By Kanchi360 Editorial · Published 30 Jan 2026 · Updated 30 Jan 2026 · 6 min read

Arriving by Train

Kanchipuram has its own railway station connected to the broader Tamil Nadu rail network, making train travel a viable option particularly for those coming from Chennai or other nearby towns. The station itself is a modest, manageable size compared to major city terminals, making arrival and onward travel fairly straightforward.

Arriving by Bus

Kanchipuram's bus stand is a major hub for both regional and long-distance bus services, and for many visitors — especially those coming from Chennai — bus travel is the more frequent, flexible option given the higher frequency of services compared to trains on this specific route.

Quick Tip: The main bus stand area is also close to a concentration of auto rickshaws, making it a convenient point to begin your temple and shopping itinerary right after arrival.

Getting From the Station/Bus Stand Into Town

Both the railway station and the main bus stand are within a reasonable auto rickshaw ride of the main temple areas and the silk shopping streets, so plan on hiring an auto immediately upon arrival rather than attempting the full distance on foot with luggage, especially in the heat.

  • Trains are a comfortable option from Chennai and other rail-connected towns, though frequency may be lower than buses on some routes
  • Buses tend to run more frequently, especially on the Chennai-Kanchipuram route
  • Both the railway station and bus stand have ready auto rickshaw availability for onward travel into town
  • If arriving with heavy luggage (e.g. for a longer wedding-shopping trip), factor this into your auto rickshaw arrangement

Which Should You Choose?

If you're coming specifically from Chennai for a day trip, buses often offer more frequent departure options throughout the day, giving more flexibility on timing. If you're coming from further afield or prefer the comfort of rail travel, check train schedules and connections in advance, since frequency can be more limited than road options on this specific route.

Is it easy to find luggage storage at the station or bus stand?

Facilities can vary and shouldn't be assumed — if you need to store luggage for a same-day return trip, it's worth checking with your hotel instead, or planning your itinerary so you're not carrying heavy luggage around temple and shopping stops.

How early should I arrive before a train or bus departure?

As a general practice in India, arriving with a reasonable buffer — particularly for buses, which can depart close to schedule — is safer than cutting it close, especially if you still need to travel from the town centre to the station or bus stand.
