Getting Around Kanchipuram: Public Transport and Auto Rickshaw Guide

Kanchipuram is compact but spread out enough that walking everywhere isn't practical. Here's how most visitors actually get between temples, hotels and the silk shopping streets.

By Kanchi360 Editorial · Published 14 Jan 2026 · Updated 14 Jan 2026 · 7 min read

Auto Rickshaws: The Main Way to Get Around

For most visitors, auto rickshaws are the simplest way to move between Kanchipuram's temples, the silk shopping streets and hotels, since the sights are spread across a town that's too large to comfortably cover entirely on foot, especially in the heat. Autos are widely available near the bus stand, railway station and major temples, and can typically be hired for a few hours at a time to cover multiple stops in one trip.

Quick Tip: If you plan to visit several temples plus the shopping streets in one day, negotiate a half-day or full-day rate with an auto driver upfront rather than paying per short trip — it's usually more economical and means you have transport waiting between stops.

Hiring an Auto for the Day

Many auto drivers in Kanchipuram are used to tourist itineraries and can suggest a sensible temple-visiting order based on timing and crowd levels, effectively acting as an informal local guide. Agree on the route and price before starting, and it helps to have a rough list of what you want to see so the driver can plan the sequence efficiently.

Local Buses

Local town buses do connect different parts of Kanchipuram and are the most economical option, but they require more familiarity with local routes and are less convenient for visitors with limited time or unfamiliar with the town's layout. They're a reasonable option if you're staying longer and want to explore at a slower, more budget-conscious pace.

Walking Between Nearby Temples

Some temples in the town centre are close enough to each other to walk between comfortably, particularly if you're already in the main temple cluster area. However, given Kanchipuram's size and the summer heat for much of the year, plan on auto rickshaws for anything beyond a short walk, especially between the temple areas and the silk shopping streets, which are a meaningful distance apart.

  • Agree on auto fares (or a half-day/full-day package) before starting your trip, as is standard local practice
  • Keep a mental or written list of stops so your driver can plan an efficient route
  • Carry small denomination cash for auto fares and local transport
  • Avoid peak midday heat for walking between nearby stops — early morning or late afternoon is more comfortable
Are ride-hailing apps available in Kanchipuram?

Coverage and availability can be inconsistent compared to larger cities, so it's sensible to have a backup plan of simply hailing a local auto rickshaw directly, especially outside the main town centre.

Is it easy to find transport late in the evening?

Auto availability drops noticeably later in the evening compared to daytime, so if you have a late train or bus to catch, it's safer to arrange transport in advance rather than expecting to hail one on the spot.
