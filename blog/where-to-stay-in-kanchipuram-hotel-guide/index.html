Where to Stay in Kanchipuram: A Practical Hotel Guide

Most visitors treat Kanchipuram as a day trip from Chennai, but staying overnight opens up a much more relaxed pace for temple visits and silk shopping. Here's how to think about where to stay.

By Kanchi360 Editorial · Published 9 Jan 2026 · Updated 9 Jan 2026 · 7 min read

Do You Even Need to Stay Overnight?

Given its proximity to Chennai, many visitors do Kanchipuram as a single-day trip and skip overnight accommodation entirely. However, an overnight stay makes sense if you're combining Kanchipuram with Mahabalipuram or Vellore, if you're here specifically for extensive wedding shopping, or if you simply prefer a slower, less rushed exploration of the temples.

Budget Hotels

Budget accommodation in Kanchipuram is generally simple and functional — clean rooms with basic amenities, often run as family businesses, catering primarily to pilgrims and traders visiting for silk business. Expect straightforward service and modest facilities rather than resort-style comforts, at correspondingly affordable rates. These are a sensible choice if you mainly need a place to sleep between temple visits and shopping.

Mid-Range Options

Mid-range hotels typically offer air-conditioned rooms, in-house vegetarian restaurants, and slightly more consistent service standards, often located a short drive from the main temple and shopping areas. This tier suits most leisure travellers and small families comfortably without a significant cost jump from budget options.

Premium and Resort-Style Stays

Kanchipuram itself has fewer large luxury properties compared to bigger tourist hubs, so travellers looking for a genuinely premium stay sometimes choose a well-rated property in Chennai or a resort near Mahabalipuram and do Kanchipuram as a day trip instead, particularly if five-star amenities are a priority.

Quick Tip: If you're visiting specifically for wedding-related shopping, ask your hotel whether they offer any tie-ups or recommendations with silk showrooms — some mid-range hotels catering to shopping tourists do.
  • Book ahead during major temple festival periods, when local accommodation fills up quickly
  • Confirm whether breakfast and vegetarian meal options are included, since pure-veg dining is the norm here
  • Ask about proximity to the silk shopping streets if that's a priority for your trip
  • Check for basic amenities like reliable hot water and air conditioning if travelling in peak summer
Is it better to stay in Kanchipuram or in Chennai and visit as a day trip?

If Kanchipuram is your main destination or you're combining it with Mahabalipuram, staying locally saves travel time and lets you start temple visits early. If you want luxury amenities and Kanchipuram is just one stop among several, basing yourself in Chennai and day-tripping is a reasonable alternative.

Are non-vegetarian food options available at Kanchipuram hotels?

Many hotels in Kanchipuram are pure-vegetarian in line with the town's strong temple culture. If non-vegetarian food is important to you, check with the hotel in advance or plan to look for specific restaurants in town rather than assuming in-house options.
