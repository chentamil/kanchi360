Common Tourist Mistakes to Avoid in Kanchipuram

A little planning avoids most of the common frustrations first-time visitors run into in Kanchipuram — from rushed itineraries to saree shopping regrets.

By Kanchi360 Editorial · Published 26 Jan 2026 · Updated 26 Jan 2026 · 7 min read

Mistake 1: Trying to See Everything in Too Little Time

Kanchipuram has more genuinely worthwhile temples than most single-day itineraries can comfortably cover. Trying to cram five or six temples plus extensive shopping into one rushed day usually means shallow visits everywhere and no time to actually enjoy any of it. Pick a realistic shortlist (see our one-day or two-day itinerary guides) rather than trying to see everything.

Mistake 2: Shopping for Sarees Without Any Research

Walking into a saree purchase — especially a wedding saree — with zero understanding of zari types, Silk Mark, or the GI tag puts you at a real disadvantage when comparing options and prices. Even fifteen minutes of reading beforehand (our buying guide and Silk Mark/GI tag guide cover the basics) makes a noticeable difference in how confidently you shop.

Warning: Rushing a major saree purchase in the last hour before you need to leave town is a common regret — leave real time for comparison shopping, especially for wedding purchases.

Mistake 3: Visiting Temples at the Hottest Time of Day

Midday sun combined with bare feet on stone temple courtyards can turn what should be an enjoyable visit into an uncomfortable one, especially in summer months. Plan temple visits for early morning or late afternoon whenever your schedule allows.

Mistake 4: Not Confirming Transport in Advance

Assuming you'll easily find an auto rickshaw whenever you need one, especially later in the evening, can leave you stranded or scrambling. If you have a late bus or train to catch, arrange transport ahead of time rather than counting on hailing one on the spot.

Mistake 5: Ignoring Dress Code Expectations

Showing up at major temples in shorts or sleeveless tops can mean being turned away or feeling out of place, particularly for first-time visitors and foreign tourists unfamiliar with local norms. Dressing modestly by default avoids this entirely (see our temple dress code guide).

Mistake 6: Not Carrying Enough Cash

Many smaller eateries, local shops and auto rickshaw fares are cash-based rather than card-friendly. Running short on cash mid-trip is a common, easily avoidable inconvenience.

  • Plan a realistic, shortlisted itinerary rather than trying to see everything in one rushed day
  • Do a little research on saree buying basics before shopping, especially for big-ticket wedding purchases
  • Visit temples during cooler morning or evening hours
  • Arrange transport ahead of time for early morning or late evening travel
  • Dress modestly for temple visits by default
  • Carry sufficient cash for local transport and smaller eateries
What's the single most common regret among first-time Kanchipuram visitors?

Rushing the trip — trying to see too many temples and shop too hurriedly in too little time is the most frequently mentioned regret, more than any specific logistical issue.

Is Kanchipuram difficult for first-time solo travellers?

Not particularly — it's a manageable, compact town with helpful locals and widely available auto rickshaw transport, as long as you plan realistically and dress/behave in line with local temple customs.
